How TTH(N₂) Fingerprints Work

The Commissioner only ever receives TTH(N₂) fingerprints — never the actual N₂ codes. This is guaranteed by the pre-image resistance property of the hash function: knowing TTH(N₂) it is computationally infeasible to reconstruct a valid N₂. After fingerprints are computed, the N₂ list is destroyed.

This prevents the Commissioner from forging valid ballots, even though they control which N₁ codes are accepted.
